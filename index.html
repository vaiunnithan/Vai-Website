01 / ABOUT ME

A little about me

I'm Vaidehi Unnithan, a Finance and Data Science student at Northeastern University. Combining the two has made me interested in more than the final number on a report. I want to understand where that number came from, how it was checked, and whether people can use it to make a better decision. I especially enjoy projects that connect technical details with a practical financial question.

In 2026, I spent eight months on the Finance Data Analytics team at Amalgamated Bank in New York. I automated more than 16 regulatory and financial reporting workflows, worked with Axiom, SQL, Excel, and VBA, and presented reporting improvements to senior finance leadership. Before that, I worked with cash flow and inventory forecasting at Subway IP, LLC and with purchasing and budgets at Morris County Vocational School District. Each experience helped me become more comfortable turning complicated information into something useful.

Outside of work and school, dance has been part of my life for years. I trained in Bharatanatyam, Kuchipudi, and Mohiniyattam, and I'm now a member of Rangila, Northeastern's fusion dance team. We rehearse, perform, and travel to competitions around the country. I also enjoy volunteering and spending time with animals. I've fostered two cats and one puppy, and I have two birds at home. I enjoy having interests beyond the classroom because they keep me curious and introduce me to new people and perspectives.
